# Show quality pass, October 2026 (P8-40)

Exploratory test of every Show feature listed in [ui.md](../ui.md) (Show sections), [pptx-suite.md](../pptx-suite.md) and
[charts.md](../charts.md) against PowerPoint's behaviour: Playwright scripts (headless Chromium, 1400 × 900 and 1024 × 768,
English and German UI) drove the web build through the journeys of the task brief, with a screenshot per step
(`shots/P8-40-*.png` in the task's worktree, not committed; before/after pictures of the worst items:
`shots/P8-40-S02-*`, `-S03-*`, `-S04-*`, `-S05-*`). Regression tests: the "Show quality pass (P8-40)" block of `packages/e2e/tests/show.spec.ts` and unit
tests next to the code (`apps/show/core/tests/shapes.rs`, `shortcuts.test.tsx`, `ShapeTabs.test.tsx`,
`ShowRibbon.test.tsx`, `canvas/slideText.test.ts`).

**Result: 19 findings** (S-20 turned out not to be a defect) – blocker 1 (deferred: server configuration, outside the app), major 3 (2 fixed, 1 deferred),
minor 10 (7 fixed, 3 deferred), cosmetic 5 (2 fixed, 3 deferred).
