## Goal
The three apps build as Android app bundles/APKs (here and in CI) and as iOS apps (CI, macOS runner), from the same Rust cores and frontends, with a mobile `Backend` that opens, saves and exports documents through the platform document pickers and the share sheet, registers the apps for "Open with" of .xlsx/.docx/.pptx, and is ready for signing from secrets. No layout work (P10-M2 does the compact UI); the apps may look like the desktop web on a phone for now.

## Decisions (do not re-open)
- Identifiers: `dev.rusuite.calc`, `dev.rusuite.write`, `dev.rusuite.show` (already the Tauri identifiers; Android `applicationId` and iOS bundle id identical). Product names "Rusuite Calc/Write/Show". Apple team `PM7LTV7M72`.
- Generated projects `apps/<app>/src-tauri/gen/android` and `gen/apple` are committed (Tauri recommendation), with the secret-dependent parts read from files that are git-ignored (`key.properties`/`keystore.properties`) or from env.
- Connectors and the AI assistant are **desktop-only** on mobile too (owner rule): `TauriBackend` on mobile exposes neither (`connectors`/`ai` absent) – Settings › AI and Connections must not appear. Print: hidden on mobile until P10-M6 (the `Backend.print` rejects with a typed "unsupported" and the UI hides the command when `backend.capabilities.print === false` – add a small `capabilities` object to `Backend`: `{ print: boolean; saveAs: boolean; share: boolean }`, web/desktop unchanged).
- Minimum OS: Android 8.0 (API 26) and iOS 15; targets `aarch64` + `x86_64` (emulator) for Android, `aarch64` + simulator for iOS (release AAB arm64 + x86_64 is fine; drop armv7).
- Version: `tauri.conf.json` `version` stays `3.141.0`; Android `versionCode` is derived by Tauri from the semver (document the formula and check that `3.1415.0` and `3.14159.0` yield increasing codes); iOS `CFBundleShortVersionString` = semver, build number = the versionCode. No version bumps.
