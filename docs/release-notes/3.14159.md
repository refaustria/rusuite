# Rusuite 3.14159

Rusuite is a small office suite with a Rust core: **Calc** (spreadsheets), **Write** (documents), **Show**
(presentations) and now **Notes** (notebooks). The apps open and save real `.xlsx`, `.docx` and `.pptx` files, run on
Windows, macOS and Linux, Calc, Write and Show also on Android and iOS and, with nothing to install, in your browser at
<https://rusuit.es/>.

**3.14159** adds the next digit of π (3.141 → 3.1415 → 3.14159 …). A separate 3.1415 was never published: this
release contains everything that was prepared for it (phones and tablets, signed installers, PDF export; see "Also in
this release") and the new app, **Rusuite Notes**. Rusuite is free and open source (MIT) – and it will stay that way.

## Highlights
- **Rusuite Notes** – a OneNote-style notebook app: sections, pages and sub-pages, rich text, checklists, tables, pictures,
  tags and a task view, free-form canvas pages with pen and highlighter, page history, full-text search. Shared
  notebooks update live for everybody with visible editors and never conflict – end-to-end encrypted, the server cannot
  read them. Import from **OneNote** (`.one`, `.onepkg`), Markdown, HTML and `.docx`. In your browser and as a desktop app.
- **Open files the way you expect (desktop)** – double-click a `.xlsx`, `.docx` or `.pptx` in Finder or Explorer, drop
  files on the window or the Dock icon, pick from the recent list; every document gets its own window.
- **Also first published here:** Calc, Write and Show on Android and iOS, signed desktop apps, PDF export and 24 store
  listings (below).

## What's new

### Rusuite Notes
- **Notebooks, sections and pages:** coloured section tabs, section groups, pages with sub-pages (three levels), drag &
  drop in the navigation pane, recycle bin (60 days), several notebooks open at once.
- **Writing:** headings, lists, checklists, quotes, code blocks, links and `[[page links]]` with backlinks, tables,
  pictures (paste, drop, resize), file attachments, tags and a tasks view across the notebook.
- **Canvas pages and ink:** a page can switch to a free canvas with text and picture boxes; pen, highlighter, eraser and
  lasso with pressure; ink follows the light/dark theme.
- **Search and history:** instant search over all pages, page history with restore and who wrote what.
- **Sharing (live, encrypted):** invite people with a link, see who is on the page and where their cursor is, keep
  working offline and merge afterwards without conflicts; remove members, stop sharing. The relay at rusuit.es only
  forwards and stores encrypted data; unshared notebooks never contact the server. Desktop: set your own relay.
- **Import and export:** OneNote notebooks (`.one`, `.onepkg`, with a report of what could not be converted), Markdown,
  HTML and `.docx` in and out; PDF and print; a `.rnotes` backup archive of a notebook with all pictures.
- **Where:** <https://rusuit.es/notes/> in the browser (notebooks stay in the browser's storage – download a backup now
  and then), and the desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux) with notebooks as folders on your computer, device keys in the
  system keyring and the AI assistant with your own key. 24 languages.
  *PM: the portal tile and the Notes downloads appear when `apps/portal/flags.json` is switched on (docs/release.md).*

### Desktop: documents and windows (Calc, Write, Show)
- **Open from the system:** double-click in Finder/Explorer, "Open with", a file dropped on the window or the Dock icon,
  or from the command line. File associations are registered as an alternative (your default programs stay yours).
  Before this, a double-click on macOS started the app with an empty document.
- **Recent files:** every document opened this way is recorded in the File view's recent list.
- **One window per document:** a second file opens in its own window (a file that is already open is brought to the
  front), File › New opens a window, closing a window asks only about that document, quitting asks per unsaved
  document. A second launch of the app hands its files to the running one.

## Also in this release (first published here, prepared as 3.1415)
### Phones and tablets (Calc, Write, Show)
- **Google Play and App Store:** Rusuite Calc, Write and Show for Android (8.0 and newer) and iOS (15 and newer) – free, without
  account, advertising or tracking. Play Store: <Play Store link> · App Store: <App Store link>
- **Compact layout:** below 900 px width (phones, narrow windows and the web app on a phone) the ribbon gives way to a top
  bar and a bottom command bar built from the same commands; dialogs and menus open as bottom sheets; targets are at
  least 44 px; the soft keyboard and the notch/gesture areas are respected. Tablets of 900 px and more keep the ribbon.
- **Files:** open `.xlsx`, `.docx`, `.pptx` from the system file picker or with "Open with" from other apps, save back
  to the same document, export and share through the system sheet.
- **Touch editing in Calc:** selection handles, pan versus select, pinch to zoom, a fill handle, a formula keyboard row
  with reference picking, compact formula bar, sheet strip and status chips.
- **Touch editing in Write:** the page fits the width on a phone (double-tap switches to 100 %), long-press selection
  with a floating bar and handles, handles for pictures and tables, a caret that stays above the soft keyboard.
- **Touch editing in Show:** a thumbnail strip, pinch and twist to scale and rotate shapes, a presentation in the same
  window with swipe navigation and an overlay, a Timing sheet for animations.
- **Export as PDF:** File › Export PDF and, on phones, Share as PDF and Print through the system print service.
  On Windows and macOS the desktop apps still open the print dialog (choose "Save as PDF" there); on Linux, Android and
  iOS the PDF is written directly.
- **Languages:** the interface in 24 languages also on the phone, with a compact-layout check at 412 px for each of them.

## Upgrade notes
- **Documents** stay standard `.xlsx`, `.docx` and `.pptx` files; everything made with 3.141 or 0.1.0 opens unchanged.
  Settings, recent files and autosave copies are kept. There was no separate 3.14159 release: if you come from 3.141, the
  notes below apply to you in full.
- **Windows – installer:** install the new version with the NSIS `…-setup.exe` (an upgrade over 3.141). The **MSI does
  not upgrade 3.141**: Windows Installer compares the first three version fields and the new numbering of the MSI
  (`3.14.159`) is formally lower than `3.141`. If you installed with the MSI, uninstall 3.141 first (Settings › Apps),
  then install the 3.141599 MSI. From 3.14159 on the MSI upgrades normally (the next releases keep the MINOR of the MSI version at 14).
- **macOS:** if the build is signed and notarized (see above), the "Not Opened" warning and the *Open Anyway* step of
  3.141 are gone: open the `.dmg`, drag the app to Applications and start it. You can check it with
  `spctl -a -vv "/Applications/Rusuite Calc.app"` ("accepted, source=Notarized Developer ID").
- **Linux – verify the download (GPG):** the packages come with `.asc` signatures and a signed `SHA256SUMS`.
  ```
  # download SHA256SUMS, SHA256SUMS.asc and your file from the release's Assets, then:
  curl -O https://rusuit.es/rusuite-release-key.asc            # public key; fingerprint on the Downloads page
  gpg --import rusuite-release-key.asc
  gpg --verify SHA256SUMS.asc SHA256SUMS
  sha256sum --ignore-missing -c SHA256SUMS
  gpg --verify <file>.asc <file>                               # a single package
  ```
  The key fingerprint is 8718 41E2 7F18 42D5 C9AD 3271 8489 ACCF 5138 3948.
- **Desktop, one window per document:** a second document opens in its own window instead of replacing the first;
  Cmd/Ctrl+N opens a new window, Cmd/Ctrl+W closes one.
- **Phones:** the apps install from the stores like any other app; no account is needed to use them.

## Known limitations
- **Notes:** a first share of a very large notebook (500 pages, 200 pictures) takes about half a minute; the pen swatch
  shows black in the dark theme (only the swatch, the ink itself follows the theme); the sync encryption code is loaded
  at start (about 114 KB compressed) and not yet on demand; browser notebooks live in the browser's storage – use
  *Download backup*. Native speakers have not yet proof-read the Notes terms in Irish, Maltese, Lithuanian (plurals)
  and the ribbon terms in Greek, Bulgarian and Hungarian. Notes has no phone or tablet app yet (planned).
- **Desktop windows (Tauri limits):** the window title bar shows no document icon to drag (no proxy icon); *Quit* from
  the Dock menu skips the per-window "save changes?" question (use Cmd+Q); a file dropped from the system onto the window of
  a desktop app is treated as a document to open, so dragging a picture file into a Write or Show page does not insert it
  there (use Insert › Picture; pasting and in-app drags work as before).
- **Not yet tried on hardware:** the phone and tablet apps were built, packaged and checked automatically and in a
  browser with touch emulation; file picker, write-back, sharing, "Open with" and PDF export have not yet been run on a
  real Android or iOS device. Expect rough edges and please report them.
- **Touch:** Write on a phone shows the page fit-width (≈ 49 % at 412 px, readable after a double-tap); Show's pinch
  zooms the slide only in Fit mode; Show's presenter view with a second window is desktop only (phones present in the
  same window); an iOS cold start with a document may need a second "Open" from Files; an export to a provider that
  refuses bookmarks leaves the document untitled (Save asks again).
- **PDF:** on Windows and macOS the desktop PDF export (Calc, Write, Show, Notes) still uses the print dialog (choose
  "Save as PDF"); on mobile the print service scales to another paper size instead of re-laying out the pages (choose the
  paper in File › Print first); an exported PDF does not enter the recent list.
- **Stores:** Google Play accepts 22 of the 24 listing languages (no Irish, no Maltese) and the App Store 18 (also no
  Bulgarian, Estonian, Lithuanian, Latvian); those stores show the English listing. The apps themselves are fully
  translated.
- **Languages:** native speakers have not yet proof-read the new touch terms in Maltese, Irish, Estonian and Latvian
  (and a few Hungarian and Greek wordings); reports are welcome.
- **Phones:** the AI assistant, cloud connections (WebDAV) and "Report a problem" are desktop features and are hidden on
  Android and iOS.
- **Windows:** the MSI does not upgrade 3.141 (see Upgrade notes).
- **Desktop:** no automatic updates; saving overwrites the file directly and does not warn when it changed on disk.
- **Files:** compatibility is tested against LibreOffice and generated reference files; checks with files made in
  Microsoft Office are done by hand before each release (docs/testing.md). No new Calc, Write or Show file features this
  release; the limitations of 3.141 (see its release notes) still apply.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/refaustria/rusuite/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Privacy
Your documents never leave your device. The website only serves the program files: no upload, no account,
no cookies, no tracking, no analytics, and a security policy that lets the web apps talk only to their own site. The
phone apps collect no data (the store privacy declarations say so). Autosave copies and recent files stay in your
browser (or in the app's folder) and can be removed in File › Privacy. Cloud connections (WebDAV) and the AI assistant
exist only in the desktop apps, are off until you set them up with your own account, and contact only the server you
configured, only when you use them. **Notes:** notebooks stay on your device until you share one; a shared notebook syncs through the relay at rusuit.es,
end-to-end encrypted, so the server cannot read it; unshared notebooks cause no traffic. Details:
<https://rusuit.es/privacy.html>.
