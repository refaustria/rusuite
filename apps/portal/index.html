Rusuite

A small office suite with a Rust core – runs in your browser and on the desktop.

Opens and saves real .xlsx, .docx and .pptx files. Nothing to install, no account, your files stay on your machine.

Your documents never leave your device: there is no server-side storage, no tracking and no analytics. Privacy details

Apps

  • Calc Spreadsheets

    Formulas with an Excel-compatible engine, cell styles, number formats and fill – saved as .xlsx.

    Open Calc in browser Desktop downloads

  • Write Documents

    Styles, lists, tables, images, headers and footers with a page view – saved as .docx.

    Open Write in browser Desktop downloads

  • Show Presentations

    Layouts, shapes, pictures, speaker notes and a presenter view – saved as .pptx.

    Open Show in browser Desktop downloads

Alpha – desktop builds for Windows, macOS and Linux are unsigned; your OS will ask before the first start.
