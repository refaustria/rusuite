Privacy

Rusuite keeps your documents on your own device. This page says what is stored where.

What this server stores

Nothing about you or your documents. The server only hands out the static program files; it has no database, no upload and no account. The only request a page makes besides loading its own files is /version.json (the build number shown in the footer). The server's web log records ordinary requests (time, address, page) like any web server; documents are never part of them.

What your browser stores

Calc, Write and Show keep autosave copies of unsaved changes and a list of recently used files (with a copy of each document) in this browser's own storage (IndexedDB), plus your language and settings (localStorage). This data is private to this browser profile on this device; it is not synced to other browsers or devices. In every app, File › Home › Privacy lets you remove these local copies, or stop keeping autosave copies. Clearing the site data in your browser settings removes everything as well.

Desktop apps

The desktop builds keep the same data in the app's data folder of your user account and make no network connections of their own.

Tracking

None. No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no fonts or scripts from other sites. A Content Security Policy makes your browser refuse connections to any other host.
